# Logo de A fuego lento

Creado con la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes. Las versiones para la app están en `public/icons/`: 192 px, 512 px y el icono de Apple de 180 px.

Prompt utilizado:

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: square mobile home-screen app icon for a personal Spanish recipe notebook named A fuego lento. Primary request: create one beautiful, distinctive and legible cooking logo. Subject: a simple white cooking pot with a lid and two graceful rising steam strokes; one small pale pink heart subtly formed by steam. Style: clean flat vector-like raster artwork with softly hand-drawn character, solid bold forms, polished contemporary identity. Scene/backdrop: full-bleed solid deep raspberry red (#a7354f) filling the entire square edge to edge. Composition: centered single white symbol occupying the middle 62 percent of the canvas, generous safe margin to survive circular or rounded-square mobile masking. Constraints: 1024x1024 square icon, no text, no lettering, no shadows, no gradients, no phone mockup, no outer frame, no watermark; no rounded transparent corners; opaque full-bleed background.
